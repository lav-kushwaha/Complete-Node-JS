#Monolith vs Microservices

Monolith and microservices are two different architectural approaches to building and deploying software applications. 
They represent different ways of structuring and managing an application's components, scalability, and deployment.

Monolithic Architecture:
Definition:
A monolithic application is built as a single unified unit. 
All the components (e.g., user interface, business logic, database) are combined and deployed together.

Microservices Architecture:
Definition:
Microservices break the application into smaller, independent services. 
Each service focuses on a specific business function, and they communicate with each other over a network.


#When to Use Each Architecture:

#Monolithic:
Best for small to medium-sized applications
Startups or early-stage projects needing simplicity
Teams focused on rapid development

#Microservices:
Best for large-scale, complex applications
Organizations needing flexibility, scalability, and faster releases
Teams capable of managing distributed systems
